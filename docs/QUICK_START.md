PalmCue · Quick start
=====================

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A built-in camera or USB webcam is all you need.

1. Run the PalmCue installer and launch PalmCue from the Start menu.
   For the portable edition, extract the whole ZIP, then open PalmCue.exe.
   Keep the _internal folder beside the application.
2. Choose Start camera. PalmCue finds an available webcam automatically.
3. In Practice, hold an open palm until the bar fills. Hold two fingers for
   next slide, or three for previous. Open your palm between commands.
4. Open your real slides in slideshow mode. In PalmCue's Present page,
   refresh the list and choose the slideshow window.
5. Choose Start presenting, then bring your slides to the front within
   five seconds. Hold an open palm to unlock. PalmCue stays minimized.

Close your fist to lock. Ctrl + Alt + Space stops presenting from anywhere.
You can also use PalmCue's icon in the system tray beside the clock.
Switching windows pauses presentation control. Return to PalmCue to start again.
Closing PalmCue quits and releases your camera.

Your index finger moves a visible pointer. Click and zoom are off by default.
Optional Showcase gestures, click, zoom, hold speed, and the gesture area are
in Preferences. The Gesture guide shows short animated examples.

No calibration is required. If your hand isn't detected, face a light and
keep your whole hand in the marked area. Use only one hand at a time.
For camera access problems, see Help in PalmCue and Windows camera settings.

Compatibility: slide commands send left/right arrow keys to the selected
foreground window. Zoom uses Ctrl + plus/minus; not all slideshow apps support
it. Pinch sends a normal click and can follow links or advance slides.
Always rehearse with your actual slides before presenting to an audience.

Your camera images stay on your computer. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
No microphone is used. The model and runtime are bundled; no setup downloads.

This build is unsigned. Public release signing and broad presentation-app
validation are still pending. Download only from a source you trust.
